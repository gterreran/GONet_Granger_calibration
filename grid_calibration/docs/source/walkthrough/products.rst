Products
========

The calibration workflow is built around persistent intermediate *products*.

Products are serialized outputs generated by each pipeline stage.

Why Products Exist
------------------

The product system is one of the core architectural features of the pipeline.

Products provide:

- caching,
- restartability,
- incremental processing,
- debugging support,
- workflow persistence,
- and reproducibility.

Without products, every calibration session would require recomputing the
entire workflow from scratch.

Product Lifecycle
-----------------

Products typically follow this lifecycle:

.. code-block:: text

    generated
        ↓
    saved to disk
        ↓
    registered in session
        ↓
    reused by downstream steps
        ↓
    invalidated if dependencies change

Product Naming
--------------

Products are stored as ``.npz`` files.

Examples:

.. code-block:: text

    202_250116_204846_grid_points.npz
    202_250116_204846_nominal_grid.npz
    202_250116_204846_modeling_results.npz

The naming convention allows products to be associated with:

- input images,
- workflow steps,
- and calibration sessions.

Per-Input Products
------------------

Per-input products generate one output per input image.

Examples:

- full-array
- grid-points

These products are usually selectable through dropdown menus in the GUI.

Singleton Products
------------------

Singleton products generate one shared product for the entire workflow.

Examples:

- averaged-grid
- nominal-grid
- bootstrapping-grid
- modeling-results

These products represent aggregated workflow state.

Product Discovery
-----------------

At startup, the session automatically searches for existing products.

Previously generated products are restored automatically whenever possible.

This allows workflows to resume seamlessly.

Stale Product Protection
------------------------

The workflow validates product continuity during startup.

If an upstream product is missing, downstream products are considered stale and
are ignored.

Example:

.. code-block:: text

    averaged-grid exists
    nominal-grid exists
    bootstrapping-grid exists

    but:

    unwrapped-grid is missing

In this case:

- downstream products are ignored,
- the workflow stops discovery at the missing stage,
- the GUI prevents inconsistent states.

This behavior protects against partially deleted workflows.

Product Contents
----------------

Products store:

- numerical arrays,
- assignment records,
- parameter dictionaries,
- fit results,
- and diagnostic information.

Some products contain plain numerical arrays.

Others contain semantic Python objects serialized through custom encoders.

Rebuilding Products
-------------------

Products may be regenerated at any time.

Rebuilding an upstream step may invalidate downstream products.

Users may also manually delete products to force recomputation.

Performance Benefits
--------------------

The product system significantly reduces workflow runtime.

Expensive stages such as:

- bootstrapping,
- dense assignment,
- and model fitting

can be reused without recomputation across sessions.

Debugging Benefits
------------------

Products make debugging substantially easier.

Users can:

- inspect intermediate stages,
- isolate failures,
- compare outputs,
- and rerun only problematic steps.

This is especially important for scientific calibration workflows.
